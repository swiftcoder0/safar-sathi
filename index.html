💛 About Safar Saathi

Safar Saathi — Khidki League is a playful travel companion concept that turns train time into small moments of discovery, friendly competition and connection. Passengers can spot landmarks, answer quizzes, solve riddles, play word games and collect journey badges whether they have a window seat or not. This website is an interactive front-end demo: route progress, rankings, points and rewards use sample data.
